# Business Inputs Required

## Pricing

- Customer levels.
- Price per meter by level.
- Quantity tiers.
- Dealer pricing.
- Promotional rules.
- Urgency charges.
- Design service charges.
- Courier charges.
- Credit rules.

## Production

- Machine names.
- Machine widths.
- Expected speeds.
- Supported job types.
- Maintenance schedule.
- Operator roles.
- Supplier list.
- Supplier pricing and capacity.

## Order Policy

- Accepted file types.
- Minimum order size.
- Approval rules.
- Payment-before-production rules.
- Reprint policy.
- Complaint policy.
- Cancellation policy.

## Accounts

- GST details.
- Invoice format.
- Receipt format.
- Credit note and debit note rules.
- Payment gateway settlement workflow.
