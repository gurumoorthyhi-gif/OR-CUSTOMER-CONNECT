# Master Roadmap

## Goal

Build the ODD RAVEN DTF customer app and operations platform in controlled phases. The customer side must stay simple, while the internal system captures the full business workflow.

## Phase Order

1. Phase 1 - MVP customer app and manual operations core.
2. Phase 2 - production automation, QC, courier, invoices, and supplier flow.
3. Phase 3 - AI tools, CRM intelligence, inventory, dealer portal, and advanced analytics.

## Foundation Rules

- Customer identity, artwork files, order history, approvals, pricing snapshots, and audit logs must be designed correctly from the start.
- Customer screens should be mobile-first and low-friction.
- Staff screens can be detailed and operational.
- The backend database is the business source of truth.
- Uploaded artwork and generated files must be private and served only through authorized access.
